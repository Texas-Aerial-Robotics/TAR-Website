Where it goes

Straight into the airframe.

We are a student organization, so there is no overhead between a donation and the bench. Here is what gifts pay for.

01

Airframes

Motors, propellers, batteries, and carbon fibre for the drones our teams design and manufacture.

02

Sensors & compute

Cameras, LiDAR, flight controllers, and the onboard computers that run our autonomy stack.

03

Prototyping

PCB fabrication, machine time, and the spare parts that make iterating on a design possible.

04

Competition travel

Registration and travel so our team can compete at the RTX Autonomous Vehicle Competition.
