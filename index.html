Who are we?

An undergraduate lab for autonomous flight.

Based in the University of Texas at Austin, Texas Aerial Robotics is an undergraduate research organization dedicated to designing, building, and programming fully autonomous drones.

Our members gain hands-on experience with industry-relevant tools and technologies, including CAD, prototyping, Linux, C++, and ROS.

What we are building this year.

Every member joins a project team and ships real hardware and real software alongside it.

Swarm

Hivemind

A fleet of small quadcopters flying as one. Every drone runs the same controller and negotiates its own role, so the formation survives losing a member mid-flight.

Read more

Folding & throwable

Origami

A drone that folds to the size of a paperback, gets thrown into the air, and unfolds mid-flight to run small jobs around a house.

Read more

VTOL

Peregrine

A tail-sitter that lifts off straight up like a quadcopter, tips over onto a fixed wing, and cruises. The hard part is the handover.

TAR at RTX

Competing on a national stage.

We are proud to have a team participating in the prestigious RTX Autonomous Vehicles Competition. This competition presents a unique challenge, where both aerial and ground robots are employed in dynamic and strategic environments.
