Who are we?

An undergraduate lab for autonomous flight.

Based in the University of Texas at Austin, Texas Aerial Robotics is an undergraduate research organization dedicated to designing, building, and programming fully autonomous drones.

Our members gain hands-on experience with industry-relevant tools and technologies, including CAD, prototyping, Linux, C++, and ROS.

What we are building this year.

Every member joins a project team and ships real hardware and real software alongside it.

01

Claw Retrieval

A drone for autonomous object retrieval and delivery using a claw mechanism, with a 2-in-1 landing gear and claw system for payload handling.

Read more

02

Thrust Vector Control Dart

A drone system that deploys a dart with thrust vectoring control via a coaxial motor, streaming live video and self-landing on deployment.

Read more

03

Color Vision Targeting

A fully autonomous Nerf gun drone system that detects and targets maroon-coloured objects using image recognition and autonomous navigation.

Read more

04

RTX Drone Competition

Our competition team builds a scout UAV that maps the arena and hands the landing zone off to a delivery vehicle.

TAR at RTX

Competing on a national stage.

We are proud to have a team participating in the prestigious RTX Autonomous Vehicles Competition. This competition presents a unique challenge, where both aerial and ground robots are employed in dynamic and strategic environments.

Explore the RAVG team

This year's mission

  • A scout UAV maps the area and finds the landing zone.
  • It communicates that location directly to the delivery vehicle.
  • The delivery UxV — air or ground — travels to the zone.
  • The package is delivered to the delivery zone.
