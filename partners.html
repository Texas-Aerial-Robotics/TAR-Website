Why partner with TAR

Your support turns into flight hours.

Every dollar and every donated part goes directly into airframes, sensors, compute, and competition travel for undergraduates learning to build autonomous systems.

01

Reach engineering talent

Our members graduate with hands-on experience in CAD, prototyping, embedded electronics, ROS, PX4, computer vision, and path planning — the exact skills teams hire for.

02

Visibility at competitions

Your logo travels with us to the RTX Autonomous Vehicle Competition, appears on our airframes and team apparel, and sits on this site year-round.

03

In-kind counts too

Software licences, components, machine time, and mentorship are just as valuable to us as funding. Tell us what you have and we will find a use for it.
