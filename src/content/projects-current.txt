Name: Hivemind
Link: hivemind
Label: Swarm
Icon: swarm
Summary: A fleet of small quadcopters flying as one. Every drone runs the
  same controller and negotiates its own role, so the formation survives
  losing a member mid-flight.
Description: A fleet of small quadcopters that fly as one. Hivemind is
  building a decentralised swarm where every drone runs the same controller
  and negotiates its own role in the air, so the formation reshapes itself
  rather than collapsing when one member drops out. Members work on the
  consensus layer, on collision-free trajectory allocation, and on a ground
  station that can task the whole fleet with a single command.
Focus areas: Multi-agent control, Consensus algorithms, ROS 2, UWB ranging,
  Formation flight, Ground station UI

Name: Origami
Link: origami
Label: Folding & throwable
Icon: fold
Summary: A drone that folds to the size of a paperback, gets thrown into
  the air, and unfolds mid-flight to run small jobs around a house.
Description: A drone that folds down to the size of a paperback, gets
  thrown into the air by hand, and unfolds mid-flight to run small jobs
  around a house. The team is designing the spring-loaded arm hinges,
  tuning a controller that has to recover a stable hover from an
  uncontrolled tumble, and teaching it to find its way around indoor spaces
  where there is no GPS to fall back on.
Focus areas: Compliant mechanisms, Spring hinges, Launch-to-hover recovery,
  Indoor VIO, Weight budgeting, Rapid prototyping

Name: Peregrine
Link: peregrine
Label: VTOL
Icon: vtol
Summary: A tail-sitter that lifts off straight up like a quadcopter, tips
  over onto a fixed wing, and cruises. The hard part is the handover.
Description: A tail-sitter that lifts off straight up like a quadcopter,
  tips over onto a fixed wing, and cruises. Peregrine is chasing the
  transition: the few seconds where the airframe stops being a multirotor
  and starts being an aeroplane. Members work on the wing, on the
  mixed-mode flight controller, and on the aerodynamic modelling that tells
  us whether a design will survive the handover before we risk it.
Focus areas: Fixed-wing aero, Transition control, CFD, Composite layup,
  PX4 airframe config, Endurance testing
