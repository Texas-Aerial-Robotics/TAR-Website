# --- The task ------------------------------------------------------------

Headline: Scout, hand off, deliver.
Overview: TAR participates in the yearly RTX drone competition. This year's
  task involves a scout Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) that will map the
  area, discover the landing zone, and communicate the location directly to
  the Delivery Vehicle, UxV (air or ground vehicle). The Delivery UxV will
  receive the location of the landing zone, fly or drive to it, and deliver
  the package to the delivery zone.
Focus areas: Aerial mapping, Landing zone detection, UAV to UxV comms,
  Payload delivery, Systems integration


# --- The mission sequence, in order --------------------------------------

Step: Map
Detail: The scout UAV launches and surveys the arena.

Step: Find
Detail: It identifies the landing zone from the air.

Step: Hand off
Detail: The coordinates go straight to the delivery vehicle.

Step: Travel
Detail: The delivery UxV flies or drives to the zone.

Step: Deliver
Detail: The package is placed in the delivery zone.


# --- The vehicles we are building for it ---------------------------------

Vehicle: Scout UAV
Icon: drone
Description: The aerial half of the system. It takes off, builds a map of
  the arena, recognises the landing zone, and transmits its location to the
  delivery vehicle.
Focus areas: Autonomous flight, Aerial mapping, Computer vision, Telemetry

Vehicle: Delivery UxV
Icon: package
Description: Air or ground. It receives the landing zone coordinates,
  navigates there while avoiding obstacles, and releases the payload in the
  delivery zone.
Focus areas: Path planning, Obstacle avoidance, Payload release,
  Vehicle-to-vehicle comms
